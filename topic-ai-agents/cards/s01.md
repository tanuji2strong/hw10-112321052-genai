## Verbatim quotes (copied from the page)

> "Workflows are systems where LLMs and tools are orchestrated through predefined code paths." [S01]
> "Agents, on the other hand, are systems where LLMs dynamically direct their own processes and tool usage, maintaining control over how they accomplish tasks." [S01]
> "When building applications with LLMs, we recommend finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed." [S01]
> "The basic building block of agentic systems is an LLM enhanced with augmentations such as retrieval, tools, and memory." [S01]
> "The autonomous nature of agents means higher costs, and the potential for compounding errors." [S01]
> "For many applications, however, optimizing single LLM calls with retrieval and in-context examples is usually enough." [S01]

## Claims (copied from the page)

- 作者 / 機構：Anthropic
- 網址：https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents
- 日期：2024-12-19
- 原始檔：[raw/01_anthropic_building_effective_agents.md](../../raw/01_anthropic_building_effective_agents.md)
- 文章區分 workflow（預先寫好的程式路徑）與 agent（由 LLM 自己決定流程）。[S01]
- 建議先找最簡單的解法，只有需要時才增加複雜度。[S01]
- 基本積木是「增強型 LLM（augmented LLM）」，也就是加上檢索、工具與記憶的 LLM。[S01]
- Agent 的自主性有代價，文章提到成本與累積錯誤的風險。[S01]
- 對於大多數應用，單次 LLM 呼叫加上檢索與範例通常就夠用。[S01]
