## Verbatim quotes (copied from the page)

> "The model never runs anything on its own." [S02]
> "It emits a structured request, your code (or Anthropic’s servers) runs the operation, and the result flows back into the conversation." [S02]
> "Claude determines when and how to call them." [S02]
> "Claude never receives your implementation; it only receives the schema you provided and the result you returned." [S02]
> "The execution model is identical to user-defined tools: the response contains a `tool_use` block, your code runs the operation, and you send back a `tool_result`." [S02]

- 工具使用是應用程式與模型之間的一份「合約」。模型自己不執行任何東西，它只發出結構化的請求。[S02]
- 由 Claude 決定何時、如何呼叫工具。[S02]
- 使用者自訂的工具（client tools）由你的程式執行。Claude 只看得到你提供的 schema 與你回傳的結果。[S02]
- 工具呼叫的流程：Claude 回傳 `tool_use` 區塊，程式執行後用 `tool_result` 把結果送回，然後模型繼續。[S02]
- 循環的結束條件：只要 stop_reason 是 tool_use 就繼續執行工具；其他停止原因（例如 end_turn）代表 Claude 已給出最終回答，或需要應用程式處理。[S02]
